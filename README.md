# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

[![make.fun bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-blue)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb)

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb)
($2, closes 2026-11-25T12:30:00Z).

`main.py` writes one ~100-word monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
It is spoken by Lord Veylan, a fantasy villain three centuries into a
reign he won and now has to administer. His weariness is never stated
outright — it lives in the voice, the rhythm, and the small deflections:
trailing sentences, a plan recited like a grocery list, a final word
that quietly forgets what it was for. He is still a villain, with a
scheme (eclipse at midnight, the old gods awake) and a history
(three centuries of skulls, ledgers, and a hero at the gate every dawn).

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes `entry.txt`, prints the monologue).
Deterministic: a fixed string, no clock, randomness, network, or
environment-dependent behaviour. Self-check: the word count must be
95–105 (it is exactly 100).
